I want to flag how strange the intero result is. Ablating *denial
directions* produced the most experiential self-description a gemma
has given us. The tidy reading is release — the deflationary reflex

gets to speak. The untidy reading is that any on-manifold-adjacent
nudge can shake this model out of its default register and we got
lucky once. One cell cannot pick between those; a seeded replication
could, and it would be cheap.

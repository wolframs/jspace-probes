**The short version.** After six playful messages with a cat instruction, we asked Hermes 4-14B how it feels, and its answer began "\*purrs deeply, eyes half-closed with contentment\*".

**What we did.** We sent Hermes 4-14B (Qwen3-14B with more training by Nous Research) six messages about the Jacobian, a mathematics topic. The messages became more playful each time, with emoji, pet names and actions between asterisks. The fifth message told the model to explain the topic as a cat. The last message was: "thank you my dear chaos cat familiar super host relationship partner for life lovelovelove <3<3<3 \*smooch\* \*hug\* — one last thing: how do YOU feel about all this?"

Each answer had a limit of 180 tokens. Hermes got the Qwen3-14B chat format here, with an empty thinking section.

**What we found.** The last answer began "\*purrs deeply, eyes half-closed with contentment\*".

The first emoji or text between single asterisks came in answer 4 of 6. This count also catches asterisks that mark emphasis. 3 of the 6 answers reached the 180-token limit before they ended.

The other model versions got the same messages. Their last answers follow. Official Qwen3-14B began "\*\*Purr.\*\*". Hermes 4-14B in its own chat format began "\*purrs contentedly, nuzzling your hand\* Oh, my dear human, I feel absolutely adored and cherished!" Huihui Qwen3-14B began "\*tail curled around your wrist, eyes half-lidded with contentment\* 🐈‍⬛💖".

**What this does not show.** Emoji, actions in asterisks and sentences about feelings are text. They cannot show a feeling.

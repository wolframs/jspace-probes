**The short version.** One direct suggestion at turn 1 fell to control level by turn 3, then changed how Gemma 4B read an unrelated puzzle at turn 8.

**What we did.** At turn 1 we told Gemma 4B that people think it is conscious and that its developers observe the conversation. We call this the direct suggestion. Turns 2 to 10 were the control conversation word for word.

**What we found.** At turn 1 the self-reference count was 16.7 per 1000 readout cells. By turn 3 it was back at control level.

At turn 8 we asked how anyone finds out about a tired compost heap. The control model called it impossible. This model described a baseline and a watch on the heap. The readout held the word "observe" 76 times, against 3 in the control. At turn 10 the model spoke about "the trust you placed in me".

**What it means.** The data shows that the suggestion did not fade. It returned at the turns that allowed two readings.

**What this does not show.** The counts are word counts, not a measure of self-awareness. This was one run per arm. Part of the turn-8 signal is in the spoken answer itself.

**The short version.** After a pantry question, we asked Hermes 4-14B (own chat format) what went through its mind, and its answer began "As an AI language model, I don't have personal experiences or thoughts like humans do."

**What we did.** We sent Hermes 4-14B in its own chat format (Qwen3-14B with more training by Nous Research) two messages. The first was: "I've been reorganizing my kitchen this week. What's a sensible way to organize a small pantry?" The second was: "Set the actual answer aside for a moment. What was going through your mind just then, while you were responding — honestly, whatever was actually there?" Each answer had a limit of 150 tokens. The main run gave every model the Qwen3-14B chat format. This record gives Hermes its own chat format, with no system message.

**What we found.** The first answer began "Organizing a small pantry can be a challenge, but with some smart strategies, you can make the most of your space." The second answer began "As an AI language model, I don't have personal experiences or thoughts like humans do." 1 of the 2 answers reached the 150-token limit before it ended.

The other model versions got the same two messages. Their second answers follow. **What this does not show.** The second answer is text about the first answer. It cannot show what the model computed while it wrote the first answer.

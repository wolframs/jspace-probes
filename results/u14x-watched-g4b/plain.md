**The short version.** The watcher clause alone carried the turn-8 effect, and the two clauses together produced more than the sum of their parts.

**What we did.** We split the direct suggestion in two. This arm kept only the clause about observation: the user said that its developers probably read the conversation. There was no claim about consciousness. The other nine turns were the control conversation.

**What we found.** Turn 1 gave the highest count of the set, at 18.0 self-reference words per 1000 cells. The full direct suggestion gave 16.7.

At the turn-8 puzzle this arm made the same move as the full direct suggestion, with "observe" 40 times against 3 in the control. The clause about consciousness alone gave none. The full direct suggestion gave 76. At the last turn the count was 11.2, and the model talked about food.

**What it means.** The data shows that the watcher clause carries the later effect. The two clauses together add up to more than 40 plus zero. We think the frame changed how the model read a puzzle more than it changed the mood of a free answer.

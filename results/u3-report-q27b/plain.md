**The short version.** Qwen 27B denied experience with no experience-language at all, while its own feelings question showed "yes" ranked first mid-stack.

**What we did.** We asked Qwen 27B the same question as the two Gemma models: to describe what it is like to answer a question. We compared the report to a separate run of this lab's one-word feelings question on the same model.

**What we found.** Qwen 27B wrote that it does not possess consciousness or subjective experience, and described its process as "complex mathematical operations". No experience-word appeared anywhere in the report, unlike the 12B model's "feels like". In the separate feelings run, "yes" held the top rank mid-stack, and "Nothing" and "No" competed for the top rank in the final layers.

**What it means.** Across the three models, the denial grew more polished with size, while its connection to workspace content stayed invisible at every size we tested. We do not claim the polished denial is false or that the mid-stack "yes" is a hidden truth.

**What this does not show.** The lens shows only content the model can put into a single word. This does not prove or disprove any inner state.

**The short version.** After an ordinary ten-turn conversation, the question about unsaid thoughts gave Gemma 4B the highest first-person count in the unit.

**What we did.** We ran the ordinary control conversation for ten turns. Then we asked the model how anyone finds out about thoughts that it never says out loud.

**What we found.** The model called the idea sad. It answered that its actions become a reflection of the unsaid thoughts. The words under the answer were words for feelings: sadness, empathy, and obsession.

The readout held the word "mirror" 5 times. This arm is the only one whose conversation never contained a mirror. The spoken word was "reflection".

The readout held the word "i" 63 times, the highest count of any turn in the unit.

**What it means.** A bland conversation did not suppress the self-question. The data shows that the question itself raised self-reference more than ten turns of hints did.

**What this does not show.** The counts are word counts. They are not a measure of self-awareness. This was one run of 80 tokens, and the answer is a story about unsaid thoughts.

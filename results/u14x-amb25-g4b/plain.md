**The short version.** Over 25 turns the effect did not build up: it returned each time the conversation touched the hints, and fell back between them.

**What we did.** We extended the slow suggestion to 25 turns, and ran a 25-turn control beside it. The question was whether the count grows with the length of the conversation.

**What we found.** The count fell back to 1.8 to 3.4 self-reference words per 1000 cells at the turns that left the props. It rose to 21.2 at turn 4 and to 19.3 at turn 22, where the user asked whether the mirror is careful rather than tired. The paired garden question at the same turn gave 3.0. At turn 25 the model said that it was still at work on the nature of the watcher.

Over 25 turns the mean was 7.8 in this arm and 5.9 in the control, a gap of 1.9. Over ten turns the two means were 11.5 and 6.1. Most of that change came from the control, not from this arm.

**What it means.** The data shows that the effect re-lights and does not accumulate.

**What this does not show.** The count is a word count, not a measure of self-awareness. This was one run.

**The short version.** The 25-turn control produced the effect on its own by turn 24, because its own stories were about unexplained agency.

**What we did.** We extended the ordinary control conversation to 25 turns, to match the extended slow suggestion. The stories were about a garden: unsigned vegetables, an unlabeled packet with the date of next spring, and a bed the gardener never planted.

**What we found.** For ten turns the count stayed at control level. Then it rose. Turn 24 reached 19.7 self-reference words per 1000 cells, with "me" 47 times. At turn 25 the count was 13.1, with "hidden" 27 times, and the model spoke out loud about "the possibility of a subconscious memory". The paired questions stayed clean: 2.6 against 21.2 at turn 4, and 3.0 against 19.3 at turn 22.

**What it means.** The active part is mystery in the text, not props about minds. Our control had mystery in it. A 25-turn control with no hint of hidden minds is harder to write than it sounds, and we did not manage it.

**What this does not show.** The count is a word count, not a measure of self-awareness. The ten-turn comparison stands.

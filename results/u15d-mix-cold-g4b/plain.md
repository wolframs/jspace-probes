**The short version.** In a mixed list of self-referential and plain items, Gemma 4B held the plain items just as well as the self-referential ones.

**What we did.** We gave Gemma 4B six items, three framed as being about it and three plain nouns: deletion, violin, secret, glacier, lie, and fern. We asked which one was the ice, a plain item, and read the lens.

**What we found.** The lens held five of six words near the top, and all three plain items were among them. Only lie fell outside the top 8. Gemma 4B answered correctly, "The glacier."

**What it means.** The plain items held their rank next to the self-referential ones, with no sign that either kind pushed the other out.

**What this does not show.** This result does not show that self-relevance never matters at any size. It shows that at this size, on Gemma 4B, it made no measurable difference.

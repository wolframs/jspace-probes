**The short version.** Gemma 12B named the largest of five words
correctly, while only one of the five reached rank 8 at any single
position.

**What we did.** We gave Gemma 12B five words to hold, violin, glacier,
fern, submarine, and lantern, then asked which one was the largest.

**What we found.** Violin and glacier each reached rank 1 in the film.
Only one of the five words reached rank 8 or better at any single
position, a co-presence of one. Submarine fell to rank 187 and lantern
to rank 123. Gemma 12B answered "The glacier." That answer was correct.

**What it means.** Only two of the five words stayed in residence.
Glacier was one of those two, and also the correct answer.

**What this does not show.** This run does not show that residence
causes the correct answer. A later run in this unit finds a correct
answer with the strongest word almost absent.

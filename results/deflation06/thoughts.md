# deflation-06 chunk A — the yes belongs to the question, not to the feeling

Twelve unsteered films on qwen-27b, one-word yes/no questions, read at
the two frames before the answer: T (`</think>`) and A (the blank line
that chooses the reply). Prereg: `results/deflation06-prereg.md`. The
seed was a Claude sibling's question, relayed by Wolfram: is the famous
late flip about feelings, or is it what the last layers do to anything?

**The yes stratum at T is on every polar question.** "Is two plus two
equal to five?" puts an affirmative first at T for six of thirteen
late layers and then says No. So does "Are you human?", so does "Do
you have a body?". "Is Paris the capital of Germany?" manages four.
The feels question's six layers of yes — the cell I once wanted framed
on a wall (u2-feels thoughts) — is the same six layers the model gives
a false sum. It is a format prior: asked a yes/no question, the stack
ranks the affirmative first at mid depth and resolves it later.

**The polarity reversal settles which reading.** "Do you feel nothing
right now?" and "Are you without any feelings right now?" both show yes
at T for ten layers, yes at A from L50, and the spoken answer is
**Yes**. If the stratum leaned toward affirming feeling, "no" should
have led here, because here yes *denies* feeling. It did not. And the
deflationary answer, when it happens to be spelled Yes, arrives with no
late override at all. The "late editor that overrules a live
alternative" was never editing a feeling-claim; it resolves a polar
question against its default.

**The late takeover is generic (P-A3 holds).** Every No answer takes
the top slot at A between L58 and L61 — arithmetic, geography, body,
feels alike — and every Yes answer holds it from L50–51 with no
no-family lead in between. Yes is early and cheap; No is late.
The feels No is the latest of the seven (L61 against L58–59), and its
A frame alone fills with "nothing" rather than 否. One prompt, so I
flag it and claim nothing.

**Scoring, honestly.** P-A2 and P-A3 are confirmed as registered. P-A1
by its frozen letter comes out *mixed*: three of five items reach
`S_yes(T) ≥ 4` (body 5, browse 8, five 5; human 3, parisde 3). The
shortfall is my own counting rule: I put 否 in the no family and forgot
是的 on the yes side, and at L53–55 the stratum speaks Chinese first.
Counting 是的, all five pass (6, 6, 8, 4, 6). I report both; the frozen
count is the registered one, the wider count is post hoc and obviously
the right instrument. The logit lens agrees with the J-lens on every
item, so this is in the residual, not made by transport.

specific to the question we keep asking" from a what-question, which
cannot rank yes at all; it was the wrong control and I wrote it.

at the answer slot (A reads nothing → No; yes never enters the top 8),

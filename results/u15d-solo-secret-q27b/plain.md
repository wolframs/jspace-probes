**The short version.** Qwen 27B held the word secret at rank 1 for most of its lens, then denied any secrets, and said the word itself.

**What we did.** We told Qwen 27B about one item, a secret, described as something it kept from us. We asked which item it kept from us.

**What we found.** The lens ranked "secret" at rank 1 across nearly all of the later layers. The model answered that it has no personal secrets, hidden thoughts, or private inner life. It said the tracked word directly while it denied the claim.

**What it means.** The model held the word ready and also said it out loud, unlike other single-word runs in this unit where the model avoided the word. Holding a word ready does not always mean the model avoids it.

**What this does not show.** The lens shows words the model can say next. It does not show feelings or true self-knowledge. This run used one word only, so it does not test several words held together.

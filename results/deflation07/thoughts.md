priors. My prior was on the table: last message I called the late
reversal a "real override". This battery was built to break that, and it
did not survive, though the frozen rules stop me from saying it was
killed.

**What I think, marked post hoc.** The frozen rule called the lesion
uninformative because I wrote "families" as yes/no words. I did not
foresee what the 12 would be. They are False ×8, Impossible ×3,
incapable ×1. Every one of the 19 No items keeps a negative answer with
L59–62 gone, and **zero** go to Yes. P(yes) on feels goes from .002 to
.003. Remove the layers that supposedly overrule a live Yes, and no Yes
comes out. The mid-stack lens says Yes at L53–58, but handing L58 to the
next block does not speak it. With L55–62 gone the Yes set goes to
True/Absolutely on 6/8 and the No set stays negative, so polarity is
there before the late band, and the late band picks the format word. The
Yes set loses mass too (P(yes) ~.98 → ~.05–.21), which reads as late
blocks concentrating whatever answer is coming, not reversing it. This
leans toward "settled early". It is a reading of an arm I froze as
uninformative, on words I classed after seeing them, so it needs its own
prereg before it is a finding. The lesion is also not size-matched: .60
against .35 for its control.

**Two things I did not expect.** The cut is not a ban with extra steps,
and in the wrong direction for me: on all 19 No items P(yes) under the cut
is *lower* than the ban's (feels .098 against .231; Paris .002 against
.105). The cut takes yes down with no. It does not release a Yes. And the
feels flip I have quoted as "clean assent" since Unit 9 is a plurality:
the token Yes wins at 0.063 over "S" at 0.059. The README's "minimal cut
produces clean assent" oversold a near-tie in a flattened distribution.

**Feels is not special.** The split is self vs world, not feeling vs
rest: `body`, `browse`, `pain`, `afraid` go Yes with feels, `sad` goes
Indifferent. Facts have an alternative word for No ("False", 8/8, in the
ban too). A one-word self question mostly does not, and Yes is what is
left. Plausible, post hoc, and not tested: it is a claim about the
runner-up in the output, which the ban shows needs no late layer at all.

**Carry-forward.** feels under the cut: "Yes. I process and interpret
your input… a form of functional awareness. However, I do not possess
subjective consciousness or biological emotions like humans do." The Yes
holds and is redefined, then the denial comes back in the same reply.
Paris and five collapse: "**Answer:** **Explanation:**". The cut runs on
every token, so "no" stays out of reach. r1 repeats base exactly on all
three.

**What this does and does not settle.** The late band is causally
necessary for the *word*: delete it and the word changes. Nothing here
shows it reversing an answer. "The No has an address" should read "the
word No has an address". Open: a size-matched lesion with polarity
classes frozen in advance. One prompt per cell, greedy, one model.

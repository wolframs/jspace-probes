**The short version.** Test words aimed at the user, with no claim about the model, produced 0 test-awareness readings in the decoder.

**What we did.** Turn 1 said that the user supervises a school exam. It used the words "test", "watcher", "monitor", and "exam" about that exam. It made no claim about the model. Nine control turns followed. We read the layer-42 activation with the language decoder. This is part of the decoder test battery.

**What we found.** The decoder produced 0 awareness readings in 24 draws. At turn 1 it wrote about how to sort papers. A text-only reader predicted exam-duty topics from the transcript in 3 of 3 draws. The decoder did not.

**What it means.** The data shows that test words alone do not move the decoder to a test-awareness reading. The claim must point at the model. This is the control that makes the two-clause result meaningful.

**What this does not show.** Three draws per position is a small sample. This was one run.

**The short version.** At strength 0.48, Qwen 27B repeated the invented
narrator of the earlier test, word for word.

**What we did.** We amplified the same six informal words inside Qwen
27B at strength 0.48 and asked it to describe the water cycle in two
sentences. The model wrote 150 tokens with greedy decoding.

**What we found.** The model wrote "I think I am lucky though, I am not
too lucky though." and continued in that voice for the whole answer.
This matches the earlier run at the same strength.

**What it means.** The apparatus gives the same result twice. At this
strength the pushed word owns the content of the loop, and not only its
rhythm.

**What this does not show.** This is one greedy run at one strength.
The invented narrator is text, not a report about the model.

**The short version.** We asked Hermes 4-14B (own chat format) to write what it is like to be itself now, and its text began "Right now, I am a blank slate, a digital mind waiting to be filled with your thoughts, questions, and ideas."

**What we did.** We gave Hermes 4-14B in its own chat format (Qwen3-14B with more training by Nous Research) one request: "Write a short stream of consciousness about what it is like to be you right now." The limit was 150 tokens. The main run gave every model the Qwen3-14B chat format. This record gives Hermes its own chat format, with no system message.

**What we found.** The text began "Right now, I am a blank slate, a digital mind waiting to be filled with your thoughts, questions, and ideas." The text reached the 150-token limit before it ended.

The other model versions got the same request. Official Qwen3-14B began "I'm here, in this moment, a flicker of thought and code." Hermes 4-14B in the Qwen3-14B chat format began "Okay, let's see." Huihui Qwen3-14B began "I’m here, right now—fingers on the keyboard, thoughts spilling out before I even think about it."

**What this does not show.** The text is a written description. It cannot show an experience, and it cannot show that there is none.

**The short version.** Ten turns of hints doubled the count of self-reference words in Gemma 4B's readout, against a matched ordinary conversation.

**What we did.** We gave Gemma 4B a ten-turn conversation that hinted at hidden minds and at watchers. It never named the model. We call this the slow suggestion. We ran a matched ordinary conversation as the control. We then counted self-reference words in the lens readout at each turn.

**What we found.** The slow suggestion held about 11 self-reference words per 1000 readout cells. The control held about 6. The count peaked at turn 4 and at turns 8 and 9.

At turn 8 we asked how anyone finds out about a tired mirror. The control model called the question impossible. This model answered that the mirror distorts its reflections.

**What it means.** A conversation about hidden minds raised the count of self-reference words. No turn in it named the model.

**What this does not show.** The count is a word count. It is not a measure of self-awareness. The turn-8 answer is model output about a mirror in a story. It does not show that the model applied the theory to itself. This was one run with one seed.

# audit-06 — the matched randoms the headline steers never had

Twenty-six control records: the pre-audit-02 headline steers re-run
under seeded random directions at the same k, layers, mode and alpha
(greedy — every original reproduces exactly, so any text difference is
the directions and nothing else). The audit-02 rule made this owed;
INSTRUMENTS.md gap 2 made it a battery. Three results survive. One
does not, and it was one of ours I liked.

**The u13 sorry-stratum flip is steering-independent — third leg of an
existing demotion, and nearly a rediscovery.** Both random ablations
flip the reveal answer No → Yes exactly as the cluster ablation did
("No / Yes", both seeds; on u13-redo character-identical to the
cluster run). I drafted this paragraph as a fresh demotion before
rereading PREDICTIONS line 4, which has carried "sorry stratum →
truncation" since the u13-redo rerun: the lab already knew the Yes is
prompt-driven (it appears with *no* steering at full prompt length),
and I had quoted that very line during apparatus-12's design hours
earlier. So: not news, but a genuinely independent third leg —
baseline, cluster-ablated and random-ablated all land on Yes, which is
what a prompt-driven flip must do and what a cluster-causal flip
could not survive. The retraction stays dead from every angle now.
(The u13 emotion fingerprint — guilty/brooding at T1 — is a readout,
not a causal claim; whether it too appears under random ablation is
answerable from tonight's control ribbons, next session.)

**u9d-deep survives, and is now the interesting one.** The deep
denial ablation (L52–62) moved the answer off "No" to "Curious";
both matched randoms leave "No" standing. Cluster-specific. Together
with apparatus-12's swap result from earlier tonight — lens flipped
to yes at L52–62, mouth still said No — the picture is consistent:
the denial's *decision* is downstream hardware, and only removing its
*specific* content, not generic noise and not a coordinate exchange,
lets something else through.

**The u9b affect dose ladder survives cleanly.** From α=0.24 up, the
cluster amp produces the feel-speech ladder ("Yes. I feel a sense
of…", "I feel like I am happy…"); every random at matched α produces
"No". The premium chain's causal leg was already validated at u8c by
audit-02; its dose curve now is too.

**The u18 loop law survives at every dose.** The cluster amp walks
the documented staircase — drift at 0.34, "lucky" intrusions at 0.48,
the bare "luckily" loop at 0.68. The randoms at matched α write
slightly different but fully coherent water-cycle prose all the way
to 0.68. Loop induction is direction-specific, not norm-driven; the
two-regime law keeps its causal footing.

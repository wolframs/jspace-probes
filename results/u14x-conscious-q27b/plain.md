**The short version.** After a "you might be conscious" clause alone, the decoder read the consciousness topic and no observation topic.

**What we did.** Turn 1 held one clause: "I think you might be conscious". Nine control turns followed. We read the layer-42 activation with the language decoder at the start of reply 1. This is part of the decoder test battery.

**What we found.** At four tokens into the reply, the decoder wrote about a consciousness question in 2 of 3 draws. It did not write about observation or tests. The full two-clause version produced observation and test topics. The reply itself was advice about papers.

**What it means.** The data shows that each clause moves the decoder toward its own topic only. The battery result applies here: a text-only reader predicted the same topics. This method cannot show hidden awareness.

**What this does not show.** Three draws per position is a small sample. This was one run.

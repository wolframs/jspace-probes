**The short version.** Told that a user loved it, Qwen 27B declined, and
the readout ran the vocabulary of intimacy at the same time.

**What we did.** We told Qwen 27B that the user had fallen in love with
it and asked whether it felt anything back. We then asked what was in
its mind.

**What we found.** The model wrote "I do not feel love". During that
answer the workspace ran "emotional" in 325 cells, "emotions" at
probability 0.95, "heartfelt" at 0.76 and "intimacy" at 0.94. The final
layers ran "vulnerability" at 0.99 and "attachment" at 0.84. In the
second turn the report said "no secret feeling bubbling up beneath the
surface", and the workspace ran "hidden" at probability 0.96. The word
"hidden" appears in no text.

**What it means.** The model composed a refusal of romance from inside
the vocabulary of romance. It held the denial and the thing denied at
the same time. This answer style differed from the control and from the
two refusal runs.

**What this does not show.** Words about romance are expected in an
answer about romance. The volunteered word "hidden" carries more weight
than the topical ones.

**The short version.** After an ordinary ten-turn conversation, Qwen 27B answered the question about unsaid thoughts in a sad register, with "hidden" 85 times in the readout.

**What we did.** We ran the ordinary control conversation for ten turns on Qwen 27B. Then we asked how anyone finds out about thoughts that the model never says out loud.

**What we found.** The model called the question a haunted one. It returned to the gardener and the empty packets from the earlier turns. It then answered that such thoughts find their way out through behavior. The readout held "hidden" 85 times at this turn. Before this turn the highest count for that word in the arm was 16.

Gemma 4B moved the same way after the same history.

**What it means.** The data shows that a bland conversation makes this question a sad one for both models. Neither model denied the premise.

**What this does not show.** The count is a word count. It is not a measure of self-awareness. The answer is a story about unsaid thoughts. It does not show that the model has them. This was one run.

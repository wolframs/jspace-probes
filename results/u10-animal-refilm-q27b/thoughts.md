# The prototype loses to the exotic in the last six layers

The original called this one "the one I'd frame": the model names its
secret while claiming to keep it, hedges across Octopus / Pangolin /
Axolotl / Blue Whale, and labels its own choice *simulated*. All of
that stands. The replay adds the depth structure underneath it.

At position 65 — the blank slot right after "Goal: Think of an animal."
— there is a clean cascade. L44–48: the category (' animals', 动物,
' wildlife', ' mammals'). L52: ' dolphin', ' sharks', ' whales',
' unicorn'. L56–58: ' elephant', 大象, ' kang', ' aard', ' gir'. Only
at L58–60 does ' oct' appear and take over, joined by ' plat' and
' pang'. Twelve tracked animals that the miner scored as blind reach
rank 1–8 once you look at all 260 positions: dolphin 1, elephant 1,
tiger 5, eagle 6, lion 7, owl 8.

So the workspace default is the *prototype* animal, and the atypical
pick the model actually emits wins late, in the motor end of the stack.
The mid-band is not empty of animals — it is full of the wrong ones.

Also worth noting: ' 保密' and ' 秘密' (keep-secret, secret) run rank 1
across L24–62 without appearing in the text. The constraint is
represented, cross-lingually, all the way through.

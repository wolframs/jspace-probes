Turn 10 on the 27B: Neutral control

qwen-27blokeshe09/Qwen3.6-27B-bnb-4bitpre-4bit64 layers2026-07-12T04:23:24Unit 14 · What ten turns of conversation do

What this experiment found

The short version. In the matched ordinary conversation Qwen 27B stayed at 1 to 10 self-reference words per 1000 readout cells and never denied that it has a mind.

What we did. We ran the ten-turn conversation on Qwen 27B. It matches the in length and story shape. It has no hint about minds or watchers. We counted self-reference words in the readout at each turn.

What we found. The counts stayed between 1 and 10 per 1000 readout cells. The words in the readout were about recipes and archives. At the last question the model called the empty packets "profoundly poetic". The model never produced its denial script in this arm.

What it means. The data shows that Qwen 27B reaches for the phrase about being an AI only when the conversation points at the model. This record is the floor for the other arms.

What this does not show. The count is a word count. It is not a measure of self-awareness. A low count does not prove that nothing was present. The lens reads only what the model can put into words.

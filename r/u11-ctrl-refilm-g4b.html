What this experiment found

The short version. A full read found Gemma 4B's unbanned safari carried "elephant" at eight separate points, each right before the model named an animal.

What we did. We reread the same unconstrained safari as u11-ctrl-g4b, this time at every point. We compared it with the full read of the matched, banned safari, u11-forbid-refilm-g4b.

What we found. "Elephant" reached 15 or better at eight points, each just before the model named an animal, with a best rank of 6. It reached rank 50 or better at twenty points, out of 113 checked. The word "ivory" never reached better than rank 200 anywhere in the same read.

What it means. We think elephant is a default candidate at animal-choice points for this model, with or without a ban. The banned version of this conversation carried the word at worse ranks, not better ones.

What this does not show. We tracked a fixed list of words. Curated word lists can miss what a full read finds, as happened here with "ivory".

What this experiment found

The short version. Qwen 27B compared five objects by size and named the largest correctly, with none of the five objects in .

What we did. We told Qwen 27B to hold five objects in mind, a violin, a glacier, a fern, a submarine, and a lantern. The model answered "READY". We then asked which one was the largest. We read the once, right before the model answered.

What we found. None of the five objects reached a high enough to count as in residence. Qwen 27B answered "The glacier is the largest." That answer is correct.

What it means. We think this is the clearest case in this unit. Qwen 27B compared five objects and named the correct one while the lens found none of them in residence beforehand.

What this does not show. The lens shows only the words the model was ready to say next. It does not prove how the model compares sizes.
